# HandNote 0.11.0

Infinite whiteboards, ink that stays exactly where you wrote it, a better shape pen and faster
navigation. Tested on the iPad over five private rounds. Notes made with earlier versions open as
before.

## Infinite whiteboard

- **New whiteboard**: the HandNote ribbon icon now asks *Handwritten note* or *Infinite
  whiteboard* (also the command **New infinite whiteboard**). One canvas without edges, dotted by
  default, zoom out to 10 %.
- **Turn a note into a whiteboard**: *Change template → Infinite whiteboard* (or the command). The
  pages are laid out from left to right as page frames, with their paper, their imported PDF /
  Word / PowerPoint / Excel page and your ink. It is one undo step back to the note with pages.
- **Page frames move with the lasso**: circle a whole frame to drag it (with its ink) or delete it.
- The page counter becomes the zoom level (tap it for *Show all* or 100 %); previous / next jump
  between frames; *Back to content* appears when you wander off.
- Export the whole whiteboard (PNG / SVG, scaled down when very large) or the current view; embed
  it in Markdown with `![[Board.handnote]]`.
- Large whiteboards draw only what is on screen and simplify when zoomed far out (50 000 strokes
  stay smooth on the desktop).

## Writing

- **The ink no longer moves when you lift the pen.** A stroke is now saved exactly as it was drawn
  on screen, and reopening a note shows the same ink. Two causes of a small jump on lift were also
  fixed: the page canvas and the live ink layer could sit half a pixel apart, and the last point
  (pressure 0 on every device) thinned the end of each stroke.
- **Smoother lines.** New strokes are smoothed along their length without lagging behind the pen:
  hand tremor is evened out, corners (横折, 竖钩) stay sharp, small loops keep their shape. Measured
  on simulated Pencil input, the wobble of a straight line drops to about a sixth.
- **Smooth outline.** The edge of a stroke is one smooth outline instead of many joined segments,
  so no small bumps where the width changes. Sharp turns are still drawn exactly; translucent ink
  still never darkens where it overlaps.
- **No exit taper added on lift**: the end of a stroke stays as you drew it.
- **Zoom up to 2400 %**, round edges at any zoom, and the page is redrawn sharp during a long pinch
  (only when that is cheap enough, so pinching stays fluid).
- Strokes written before 0.11 look exactly as before.

## Shape pen

- No dashed preview while drawing; the shape replaces the ink right after you lift the pen.
- **Casual straight lines** are recognised at any angle, also when drawn slowly and lightly
  (recognition now uses the smoothed ink, not the raw jittery points).
- **U, V, S, J and the arm of a "k"** are recognised: open bends become curves you can edit;
  recognition works in screen pixels, so it behaves the same at 800 % as at 100 %.
- **Tilted ellipses and rectangles** keep their tilt.
- **Pentagons, hexagons … (5–8 sides)** are recognised and snapped to a regular polygon when the
  sides and angles are nearly equal.

## Highlighter

- A nearly straight highlighter stroke becomes an exactly straight line. Can be turned off in the
  editor's ⋯ menu or in Settings (*Straighten highlighter lines*).

## Navigation

- **Hand tool** in the toolbar (H on the desktop): pen, mouse and finger only move the page.
- **Every tool and page action is a command**, so you can give it a hotkey in Settings → Hotkeys.
- **Go to page**: tap the page counter (5/6) and type a number; imported pages show their page in
  the original document.
- **PDF contents**: a PDF with bookmarks gets a *Contents* tab in the page sidebar.
- **Pull to add a page**: keep pulling past the bottom of the last page until the circle fills,
  let go, and a page is added (setting *Add a page by pulling past the last page*).

## Settings

- Grouped into *New notes*, *Writing* and *Images, documents and embeds*; *Recognition and AI*
  and *Advanced* are folded away. All labels are translated. No setting was removed and your values
  are kept.

## Compatibility

- Whiteboards and the new curve shapes use file format 7: HandNote 0.10 and older open them
  read-only. Notes with pages are written in the same format as before.
- New strokes also store the drawn ink beside their points, so files with new handwriting are
  about 1.6–1.9 times larger. Older HandNote versions still open and edit them (they redraw the ink
  from the points).

## 中文说明

- **无限白板**：点左侧 HandNote 图标选「无限白板」新建，默认点阵、没有边界、可缩小到 10%。分页笔记可以一键转成白板（各页从左到右排成页面框，保留纸张、导入的文档和字迹），一步就能撤销回去。套索圈住页面框可以整体移动；可导出整块白板或当前视图，可嵌入 Markdown。
- **松手字不再动**：笔画按屏幕上画出来的样子保存，重新打开也一样；修好了画布差半个像素、最后一个点压力为 0 使末端变细这两个松手跳动的原因。
- **线条更顺滑**：新写的字沿笔画平滑，不落后笔尖，拐角保持尖锐；笔画边缘改成一条平滑轮廓，没有小折角；松手不再加出锋。
- **最大放大到 2400%**，放大后边缘仍是圆的，双指缩放时也会适时重画清楚。
- **形状笔**：去掉画时的虚线预览；随手画、轻轻慢慢画的直线都能认出；能认 U、V、S、J 和 k 的右半边；斜椭圆、斜矩形保持倾斜；5–8 边形能认出并自动变成正多边形。
- **荧光笔**：画得差不多直会自动变成直线（可关闭）。
- **导航**：抓手工具（电脑按 H）；所有工具和页面操作都能设快捷键；点页码跳转；PDF 书签目录；在最后一页继续往下拉添加页面。
- **设置页**：分成新建笔记、书写、图片文档和嵌入三组，识别与 AI、高级折叠起来，全部中文；没有删除任何设置。
- **兼容性**：白板和新的曲线形状使用文件格式 7，0.10 及更早版本只读打开；新写的字多存一份墨迹，文件约大 1.6–1.9 倍，旧版本仍能打开编辑。
