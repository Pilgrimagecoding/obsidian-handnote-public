# HandNote 0.11.1

Smoother handwriting on the iPad, pointed stroke ends, Apple Pencil tilt, pressure calibration for
every pen, and page bookmarks. Notes made with earlier versions open as before, and strokes written
before keep their look.

## Writing

- **Straight lines no longer look pieced together on the iPad.** iPadOS often hands HandNote the
  Apple Pencil points of one frame twice. Both copies were used, so the ink stepped back and forward
  about 120 times a second, which showed as small steps along the edge of long strokes. Repeated
  points are now ignored. In one real note, half of all points were such repeats.
- **Pointed stroke ends (笔锋) are back.** A quick flick ends in a sharp point, a slow stop stays
  rounder. The point never reaches past the ink you saw while writing; it only sharpens the round
  end. Strokes also start slightly narrower and grow to full width.
- **Smoother edges.** The width is smoothed over a longer stretch, and the two sides of a stroke are
  drawn as curves, so there is no corner at each outline point (on screen and in SVG export).
- **Light writing is as smooth as firm writing.** Single pressure dropouts of a light touch are
  filtered out, and light strokes are smoothed a little more.
- **Pen tilt.** A tilted Apple Pencil writes wider: new setting *Tilt widens* in the pen settings
  (default 40 %, so +20 % at 45°; 0 turns it off). The highlighter, the mouse and pens without tilt
  data are not affected.
- **Pressure calibration for every pen.** *Calibrate from my writing* (pen settings → Advanced) no
  longer needs the input diagnostics: write a few lines, tap it, and your typical and heavy pressure
  are measured from your last Pencil strokes. The fountain pen, pencil and ballpoint pen all use it.
- **The pencil and ballpoint pen reach their set width.** They assumed a fixed typical pressure of
  0.3, far above real Pencil writing (about 0.06–0.12), so they wrote at about half their width.
  They now use the calibrated pressure (0.12 until you calibrate). **The pencil writes thicker than
  in 0.11.0**; lower its width if you preferred it thinner.

## Page bookmarks

- **Bookmark a page** with the bookmark at the top right of its thumbnail in the page sidebar
  (it turns red), with *Add to favorites* in the editor's ⋯ menu or a thumbnail's ⌄ menu, or with
  the command *Bookmark / unbookmark the current page*.
- **All pages / Bookmarks only**: the button at the top of the page sidebar lists only the
  bookmarked pages; tap one to jump to it.
- Bookmarks are saved in the note and sync with it. They are not undo steps. They move with their
  page; whiteboards have no page bookmarks.

## Compatibility

- A bookmark is one optional field on the page (`"bookmark": true`); the file format version is
  unchanged. HandNote 0.11.0 and older open and edit these notes normally but do not show the
  bookmarks, and drop them when they save.

## 中文说明

- **iPad 上直线不再一节一节的**：iPadOS 经常把同一帧的 Apple Pencil 采样点送两遍，以前两份都用了，笔迹每秒往回跳约 120 次，长笔画的边缘出现小台阶。现在重复的点会被丢掉（一份真实笔记里一半的点是重复的）。
- **笔锋回来了**：快速甩出收成尖，慢慢停笔保留圆一些；笔尖不会超出书写时看到的墨迹，只是把圆头削尖。起笔也从稍细长到正常宽度。
- **边缘更平滑**：宽度沿笔画平滑得更长，笔画两侧改成曲线，轮廓点处没有折角（屏幕和 SVG 导出一样）。
- **轻轻写也平滑**：轻压时偶尔掉下去的压力会被滤掉，轻写的笔画平滑得更多一点。
- **笔斜着写会变宽**：笔设置里新增「倾斜加宽」（默认 40%，笔斜 45° 时宽 20%，设为 0 关闭）。荧光笔、鼠标和没有倾斜数据的笔不受影响。
- **所有带压感的笔都能校准**：笔设置 → 高级设置里的「根据我的书写校准」不用再先打开输入诊断，写几行字后点一下即可；钢笔、铅笔、圆珠笔都用这个校准值。
- **铅笔、圆珠笔达到设定的粗细**：以前它们按固定的常用压力 0.3 计算，远高于 Pencil 实际书写的 0.06–0.12，只画出一半左右的宽度；现在用校准值（没校准时 0.12）。**铅笔会比 0.11.0 粗**，觉得太粗可以调细。
- **页面收藏**：点页面栏缩略图右上角的书签（变红），或在 ⋯「更多」、缩略图的 ⌄ 菜单里选「添加到收藏夹」，也可以用命令「收藏 / 取消收藏当前页」。页面栏顶部可以切换「所有页面 / 仅书签」。收藏随笔记保存和同步，不算撤销步骤，跟着页面移动；白板没有页面收藏。
- **兼容性**：收藏只在页面上多一个 `"bookmark": true`，文件格式版本不变；0.11.0 及更早版本能正常打开和编辑，只是看不到收藏，保存后收藏会丢失。之前写的字保持原样。
