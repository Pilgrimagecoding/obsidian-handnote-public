# HandNote 0.8.0

First public release of HandNote: handwritten notebook pages for Obsidian, made for the Apple
Pencil and working with touch and mouse.

- **Notebook pages** in `.handnote` files: paper sizes and patterns, many pages per note, a page
  sidebar, embeds in Markdown (`![[Note.handnote]]`).
- **Pens** with Apple Pencil pressure, highlighter, shape pen, laser pointer; precise, partial or
  whole-stroke eraser; text boxes, images, shapes and connectors, formulas (LaTeX), mind maps.
- **Lasso**: move, resize, rotate, copy, recolour, group; a loop around part of a converted text
  picks just those characters.
- **Convert handwriting to text on your device** (Chinese and English, PaddleOCR PP-OCRv5): in
  place, one undo step, with candidates and "Restore ink". The recognition runtime (ONNX Runtime
  Web) is built into the plugin; the models (15.8 MB Chinese, 7.5 MB English) are downloaded only
  when you ask, checked by SHA-256, kept on the device and work offline. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Formulas**: type LaTeX with a live preview (Convert → Math opens the editor), or recognise
  handwritten math with MyScript using your own keys. This version has no on-device math model.
- **Optional services with your own keys**: MyScript; AI providers for mind maps, flowcharts and
  picture text — always after a confirmation.
- **Safe storage**: sync-conflict detection, recovery journal, backups; damaged or newer-format
  notes open read-only.

Requirements: Obsidian 1.11.4 or newer. On-device recognition on iPad / iPhone needs iPadOS / iOS
16.4 or newer. Network use and privacy: see README and PRIVACY.md.
